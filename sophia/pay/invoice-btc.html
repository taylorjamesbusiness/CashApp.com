How to make a BTC Lightning payment from your Cash Balance:
1
Open Cash App: Tap the Money (or '$') tab at the bottom-left corner of the app's home screen.
2
Open the QR Scanner: Tap the QR Scanner icon located at the top-left corner of the screen.
3
Scan the QR Code or Paste the Invoice: Hold your camera over the Lightning QR code we provided. If you have trouble scanning, you can copy the invoice address and use the paste option.
4
Select the Funding Source: When the payment screen appears, select your Cash Balance (USD) as the source.
5
Confirm: Complete the payment using your Cash PIN or Face ID. Cash App will automatically deduct the dollar amount from your balance and send it to My address via the Bitcoin Lightning Network.
